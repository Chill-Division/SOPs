## Introduction

These advertising SOPs have been written for a Cultivator. They provide guidance
around what you may / may not say / share. These are not intended to be an
exhaustive list, but should be thorough enough to give a licensed cultivator a
good idea around what is and is not acceptable. The goal should always be to err
on the side of caution.

Medsafe have stipulated they will always take an "education-first" approach rather
than an enforcement-first approach. However, that is a lot of goodwill that has
been afforded to the medicinal cannabis industry as a whole that you would be
wise not to abuse. If it's blatant, they are still within their rights to come down
on you like a hammer.

> **No AI use.** These SOPs were written by a human. The underlying framework
> for displaying and formatting these SOPs online has been done with AI, but not
> the content itself. We won't accept commits, contributions or suggestions from
> LLMs. There is a lot of nuance in all of these, and the last thing that we need
> is some AI suggesting cultivation facilities are run under GACP / GMP or other
> such mistakes, due to a lack of that context-specific awareness.


All terminology follows [RFC 2119](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc2119)
(MUST / SHOULD / MAY).
